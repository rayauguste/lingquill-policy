Privacy Policy — Lingquill (Chrome Extension)

Last updated: March 20, 2026

This policy describes how the Lingquill browser extension (“the Extension”) handles information when you install and use it. The Extension is provided by Ray Auguste (“we”, “us”).

Contact: raekwon322@gmail.com

Summary

  • The Extension does not sell your personal information.
  • Your Anthropic (Claude) API key is stored only on your device in Chrome’s local storage. We do not receive it on our servers (this project has no publisher-operated backend for the Extension).
  • When you use translation or daily-practice features, text you select or prompts derived from your use are sent from your browser to Anthropic’s API using your API key, subject to Anthropic’s policies and your agreement with Anthropic.
  • Saved vocabulary, progress, and settings stay on your device unless you use browser sync/backup features outside this Extension’s control.

Information the Extension processes

1. Settings (stored locally)

Stored in chrome.storage.local on your device, including:

  • Extension on/off preference
  • Learning language selection
  • Your Anthropic API key (if you choose to enter one)

The API key is used only to authenticate requests from your browser to https://api.anthropic.com as implemented in the Extension. It is not transmitted to us.

2. Text you select on web pages

When the Extension is enabled and you select text on a webpage, that selected text may be sent from your device to Anthropic to generate translations, readings, and grammar explanations. We do not operate the servers that process that request; Anthropic does.

You should not select highly sensitive text (passwords, government IDs, private medical or financial details, etc.) if you are not comfortable with that content being included in an API request to Anthropic under your account.

3. Learning data (stored locally)

The Extension may store, per learning language:

  • Saved vocabulary items (e.g. translations, notes you chose to save)
  • Daily practice suggestions and related metadata (e.g. recent prompts to reduce repetition)
  • UI preferences (e.g. which sections you expanded in the popup)

This data remains on your device in Chrome storage unless you remove the Extension or clear site/extension data.

4. Clipboard (optional, on your action)

If you use Copy in the Extension UI, the Extension may write content to your system clipboard using the browser’s clipboard APIs or a fallback copy method. That happens locally on your device.

Third-party services

Anthropic (Claude API)

The Extension sends requests to Anthropic at api.anthropic.com when you use features that require AI output. Processing is governed by Anthropic’s terms and privacy policy. You provide your own API key; usage and retention on Anthropic’s side depend on your Anthropic account and their documentation.

Websites you visit

The Extension includes content scripts that run on pages you browse so it can respond to text selection. It does not change our access to your data—we do not run infrastructure that receives your browsing data. Page content is processed in your browser except when you trigger a feature that sends selected text to Anthropic as described above.

Data retention and deletion

  • Data stored by the Extension in Chrome local storage persists until you uninstall the Extension, clear extension data, or use Chrome features that remove that storage.
  • To stop sending text to Anthropic, disable the Extension or remove your API key in Settings.

Children’s privacy

The Extension is not aimed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). Parents and guardians decide whether minors should use it and should supervise browser use (including any API key stored on the device).

This Extension does not run a signup service or hosted accounts—there is no central database for us to search or erase. To stop use or remove locally stored data, uninstall the Extension or clear extension data in Chrome. If a minor’s text was sent to Anthropic using your API key, that is handled under your Anthropic account and their policies, not by us.

International users

If you use the Extension from outside your home country, your information may be processed by Anthropic in accordance with their policies and your use of their service.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Extension changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
